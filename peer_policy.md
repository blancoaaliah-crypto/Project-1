
# FIN 439 Project 1 — Comparable-Company Policy

## Purpose

Use relative valuation as an independent perspective on Ulta's DCF value.

## Peer selection criteria

The peer group should include publicly traded operating retailers with:

- Meaningful beauty or personal care exposure
- Positive EBITDA
- Positive earnings for P/E comparisons
- Reliable and comparable financial reporting
- Comparable merchandising or retail economics
- Consistent share-price observation dates

## Initial candidates

### Sally Beauty Holdings (SBH)

Beauty retail and distribution business.

Potentially useful because of industry exposure, although its business mix, scale and operating economics differ from Ulta.

### Bath & Body Works (BBWI)

Personal care and fragrance retailer.

Useful as a secondary benchmark, but its business model and product categories differ from Ulta.

## Enterprise-value multiple

EV/EBITDA = Enterprise Value / EBITDA

Enterprise value should use a consistent treatment of:

- Equity capitalization
- Interest-bearing debt
- Cash and equivalents
- Preferred equity
- Minority interests
- Operating leases

Ulta's simplified DCF excludes operating leases from the financing bridge. Comparable-company definitions must be reconciled to that treatment.

## Equity-value multiple

P/E = Equity Market Value / Net Income

The forecast period for peer earnings must match the period used for Ulta's projected earnings.

## Exclusions

Do not include:

- Companies with incompatible financial businesses
- Negative-earnings companies in a positive P/E comparison
- Unverified market prices
- Companies with major accounting inconsistencies
- Transactions with unsupported control premiums or synergies

## Interpretation

Comparable-company valuation should not be mechanically averaged with DCF.

Differences may reflect:

- Growth expectations
- Margins
- Product mix
- Scale
- Inventory turnover
- Capital expenditures
- Investor risk expectations
- Terminal value assumptions
