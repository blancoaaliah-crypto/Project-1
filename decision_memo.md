
# FIN 439 Project 1 — Investment Committee Memo

## Ulta Beauty Inc. (NASDAQ: ULTA)

### Decision under consideration

Should the fund initiate a position in Ulta Beauty, place the company on watch, or do not initiate?

The fund currently holds no position.

## Preliminary recommendation

**Do not initiate at the observed October 16, 2026 market price, pending updated financial evidence.**

This is a provisional judgment based on the current model assumptions and is not the final investment committee recommendation.

## Investment context

Ulta Beauty operates in specialty beauty retail.

The business is affected by consumer spending, retail competition, product mix, store economics, inventory management, and capital investment requirements.

The valuation model projects five fiscal years from FY2026E through FY2030E.

## Historical basis

FY2025 net sales: $12,392.820 million.

FY2025 gross profit: $4,845.224 million.

FY2025 operating income: $1,532.992 million.

FY2025 net income: $1,153.479 million.

The base financial statements are taken from the FY2025 Form 10-K and require a refreshed valuation-date comparison.

## Valuation approach

The primary method is a five-year FCFF discounted cash flow model.

The analysis uses:

- Forecast revenue growth
- Gross margin
- SG&A
- Inventory requirements
- Depreciation and amortization
- Capital expenditures
- Income taxes
- WACC
- Terminal growth

The DCF is calculated using forecast cash flow generated from the five-year financial model.

## Preliminary valuation range

| Scenario | Estimated value per diluted share |
|---|---:|
| Downside | $286.39 |
| Base | $393.71 |
| Upside | $463.74 |

These estimates are scenario outputs from the current simplified financial model.

They should not be interpreted as precise future stock prices.

## Base case

The base case assumes approximately 5% annual revenue growth, historical gross margin and SG&A ratios, $435 million annual capital expenditures, a 9.1% WACC, and 2.5% terminal growth.

The current base valuation is approximately $393.71 per diluted share.

## Downside case

The downside case combines slower revenue growth, lower gross margins, higher SG&A intensity, greater inventory requirements, and a higher WACC.

The resulting valuation is approximately $286.39 per diluted share.

This illustrates how operational challenges and higher discount rates could adversely affect estimated intrinsic value.

## Upside case

The upside case assumes stronger revenue growth, improved gross margin, lower SG&A intensity, better inventory productivity, and a lower WACC.

The resulting valuation is approximately $463.74 per diluted share.

## Market comparison

The referenced October 16, 2026 closing price is $592.94.

Source: MarketWatch, October 16, 2026.

This market observation is later than the model's FY2025 historical financial basis.

The comparison therefore highlights a potential valuation gap, but cannot establish a fully current fair value without refreshed financial statements.

## Relative valuation

Sally Beauty Holdings and Bath & Body Works have been identified as initial peer candidates.

The project contains the methodology for EV/EBITDA and P/E analysis.

However, contemporaneous and consistently defined peer multiples are needed before relative valuation can serve as independent corroboration.

The committee should not mechanically average DCF and comparable-company estimates.

## Key investment risks

- Slower consumer spending
- Increasing retail competition
- Promotional pressure
- Declining gross margin
- Rising labor and marketing expenses
- Inventory accumulation
- Increased capital expenditures
- Higher cost of capital
- Model dependence on terminal value

## Potential reversal conditions

The provisional do-not-initiate judgment could change if updated evidence supports:

1. Higher sustainable FCFF than currently modeled.
2. Improved long-run operating margins.
3. More favorable working-capital trends.
4. A defensible lower cost of capital.
5. A material stock-price decline relative to verified intrinsic value.

An initiation recommendation should require an appropriate margin of safety.

## Monitoring triggers

Review:

- Quarterly comparable sales
- Gross margin
- SG&A as a percentage of sales or gross profit
- Inventory growth and turnover
- Cash flow from operations
- Capital expenditure guidance
- Share repurchases and dilution
- Market risk-free rates and WACC
- Current market price

## Conclusion

Based on the current scenario analysis, initiating a position at the observed October 16, 2026 price is not supported by the simplified FCFF DCF.

The appropriate provisional action is do not initiate and revisit the name after updating financial statements, peer valuation evidence, and cost-of-capital assumptions.

The decision remains conditional because the historical model basis and observed market date are not aligned and some independent validation requirements remain outstanding.
